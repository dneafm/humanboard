Discovery framework

1 · Money path
  • Who pays?
  • What are they paying for?
  • What happens before and after revenue?
  • Where do delay, leakage, churn, or loss appear?
2 · Workflow map
  • Intake
  • Triage
  • Execution
  • Review
  • Delivery
  • Feedback
3 · Friction
  • Waiting
  • Retyping
  • Tool switching
  • Guessing
  • Repeat errors
  • Hidden spreadsheet/chat work
4 · Work type
  • Data cleanup
  • Decision support
  • Approval
  • Execution
  • Monitoring
  • Exception/reporting

Core role path

Workflow reader
  • Understands how value and work move
  • Maps the real process
  • Finds hidden spreadsheet/chat work
Bottleneck finder
  • Spots delays, rework, exceptions, misses
  • Finds repetitive pain worth paying to fix
  • Separates core flow from edge cases
Tool builder
  • Builds the smallest useful operator layer
  • Creates queues, dashboards, routers, review surfaces
  • Keeps fallback and safety boundaries
Operator
  • Runs the parallel transition period
  • Works back and forth with the decision-maker
  • Hands off or maintains the system

Workflow & pain discovery

Walkthrough
  • “Walk me through what happens from the moment work comes in until it’s done.”
  • Look for inbox, spreadsheet, chat, CRM, dashboard, docs, handoffs, approvals, exceptions.
Pain signals
  • “We do this manually every day.”
  • “Only one person knows how.”
  • “We keep missing things.”
  • “This lives in spreadsheets/chat.”
  • “When volume spikes, it breaks.”

Best 5 questions: Walk me through a normal day · What takes the most time? · What do you keep checking manually? · Where do mistakes or misses happen? · If you could remove one painful step, what would it be?
